# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who click a link, play for five minutes, and decide whether to come back.

## Product Purpose

A memory game. Study paper targets, then fire one reflected laser from memory. Success is that a stranger understands the loop from the first level and wants another run.

## Positioning

Levels are generated along a real traced path and verified by the same beam code the player fires through, so nothing ships unsolvable.

## Operating Context

Browser, mouse or touch or keyboard. Two modes: ten-level Classic with three mistakes, and Endless with no mistake budget. Difficulty is a five-stop slider baked into the seed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One shot per level. Quota is 60% of the targets.
- Difficulty is a ceiling: levels climb the ramp and stop where the slider says.
- Mirrors, rulers and absorbers change the route from the middle of the curve.
- Assists are a run resource: three total, not three per level.
- No codes or links: a run is self contained, and replay keeps the same fields.
- Fully synthesised audio, no sound files.
- No backend. High score is localStorage.

## Brand Commitments

The name LaserBlink and the cardboard field are fixed. Copy stays short, no instructions on the field, no labels that repeat what the numbers say.

## Product Principles

- Playable in one click from the menu.
- Never show a level that cannot be solved.
- Say it with the field, not with text.
- Leaving and replaying both cost one action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced motion is a toggle and a system preference. States carry a shape as well as a colour. Keyboard covers every action.
